4. Important Notes

  • loading="eager" requests normal, immediate loading.
  • loading="lazy" defers loading until the resource is near the viewport.
  • Lazy loading can reduce unnecessary initial network requests.
  • Do not lazy-load important images visible immediately at the top of the page.
