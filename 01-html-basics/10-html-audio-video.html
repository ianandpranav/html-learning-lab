HTML Audio and Video

Learning how to embed multimedia content in HTML.

Audio Example

The audio element can play sound files in a browser.

Video Example

The video element can display videos with playback controls.

Important Attributes

  • controls: Displays playback controls.
  • autoplay: Requests automatic playback.
  • loop: Repeats playback.
  • muted: Starts without sound.
  • preload: Suggests how the browser loads media.
