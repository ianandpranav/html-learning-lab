HTML Progress and Meter

Learning how to display progress and measured values.

Progress Element

Shows how much of a task has been completed.

70%

70% completed

Meter Element

Shows a value within a known range.

65%

65% of storage used

Another Progress Example

4 out of 10

4 out of 10 tasks completed

Difference Between Progress and Meter

  • progress: Represents completion of a task.
  • meter: Represents a measurement within a defined range.
